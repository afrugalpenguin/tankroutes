--- Dungeon map layouts ---
Data\Maps.lua contains dungeon room layouts (room picture fileIDs, positions, sizes,
rotations and floor levels). They come from Forever Simple World Map 0.30.2 by Tarolg,
which OneForAll records as licensed under the MIT License below, and were taken from
the data file of OneForAll by Smoergi (GPL-3.0-or-later), which includes that MIT data.
Only dungeons whose layouts come from the MIT version are included. The room pictures
themselves are the game client's own minimap images and belong to Blizzard Entertainment.
